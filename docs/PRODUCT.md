# Alpha Kids — Product

## What it is

Alpha Kids is a parent coach desk. A parent types a concern in English or Urdu and gets a short, structured, safe plan plus a follow-up check-in.

It is not a doctor. It is not a lawyer. It is not for emergencies.

## Who it is for

Parents who want a practical next step for everyday parenting friction: sleep, tantrums, school mornings, sibling fights, and similar.

## Voice

Sound like a calm, practical parent friend. Short. Concrete. What happens next.

- No em dashes.
- No corporate warmth or fake profundity.
- Prefer facts and actions: "Type what's going on", "This is not a doctor", "Come back in 20 minutes".

Home is a **magnetic family wall**: bold mint field, oversized Alpha Kids wordmark, sticky-note concerns, magnet plan cards, and a kitchen-timer check-in. English and Urdu. Not a chat app.

## Safety

- No diagnosis.
- No medical or legal instructions.
- If the parent describes emergency, abuse, or self-harm: escalate. Clear redirect to local emergency / trusted help. Do not keep coaching as normal.

## Non-goals

- Login
- Database / message persistence
- Real LLM replies (mock only for now; see `docs/TECH.md`)
- Region-specific crisis hotline databases (unless we add real numbers later)
